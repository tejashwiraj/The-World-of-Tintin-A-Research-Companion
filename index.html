A Companion Map to Tintin's Journeys

From the MA Project: "The Hero's Journey in a Modernist World"

By Tejashwi Raj
Supervised by Dr. Ananya Ghoshal

An interactive companion to the MA research project, exploring modernity, travel, technology, objects and visual culture in The Adventures of Tintin.

RESEARCH QUESTIONS

1. How does Tintin represent modernity through movement, technology, objects and visual form?

2. How does Tintin represent the Hero's Journey while exploring absurdist and existentialist elements throughout his journey?

3. How do machines and technology support the rhythms of movement, travel and twentieth-century modernity?

4. How is Tintin adapted across different media and forms, representing the broader topography of popular culture and visual culture?
